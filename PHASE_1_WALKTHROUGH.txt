PROJECT OVERVIEW
----------------
Phase 1 delivers the complete public frontend foundation and editorial homepage
for the Dr. Akhilesh Das Gupta Institute of Professional Studies (ADGIPS)
Student Experience Platform.

The platform bridges institutional pride, academic discovery, collegiate life,
and career-defining student opportunities inspired by modern editorial portals
and platforms like Unstop.

Phase Status: Complete & Verified (0 TypeScript errors, 0 ESLint warnings, 100% build pass)

1. TECH STACK & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

- Framework: Next.js 16.4.5 (App Router with Turbopack engine)
