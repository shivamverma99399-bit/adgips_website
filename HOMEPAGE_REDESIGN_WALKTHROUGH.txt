1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & REDESIGN OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The initial homepage implementation was functional but suffered from visual monotony:
- Overly generic, repeating 3-4 card grids across every section
- Underwhelming hero presentation that lacked institutional stature
- Dark murky gradient overlays (bg-slate-900, from-slate-950/80) that cloaked images into gloomy, dark rectangular voids
- Dashboard-like feeling rather than an art-directed, living university digital experience

THE SOLUTION:
A complete editorial visual redesign inspired by SiteReel and premium Framer websites, embodying the design concept "ADGIPS — A Living Campus":
- Monumental typography with serif italic accents for an editorial university identity
- Asymmetric layouts breaking the uniform card rhythm
- Clean, luminous photographic treatments (no dark black scrims or murky containers)
- Architectural segmented indexes replacing generic card rows
- Deep institutional credibility rooted in official ADGIPS facts (8.08-acre Shastri Park campus, 23,636 m² built-up area, 1 Gbps fiber backbone, 44+ LPA top placement package, 85,000+ volumes library)
